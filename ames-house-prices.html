Ames House Price Prediction

Our two-person team used 1,460 labelled home sales and 79 candidate predictors to estimate prices for 1,459 test houses. Across five submitted model versions, our Kaggle log-RMSE fell by 20.0%, from 0.13868 to 0.11101.

What we tested

Fit preprocessing inside each fold
Each training fold supplied its own imputation, scaling and clipping parameters. For a missing garage or basement rating, we checked the recorded area to distinguish an absent feature from an incomplete description.
Blend models with different errors
Ridge and Gradient Boosting missed the actual price in opposite directions on 393 of 1,460 out-of-fold predictions. Averaging their log predictions produced the largest single jump in our submissions, from 0.13868 to 0.12731.
Inspect the cases driving the improvement
Capping extreme SVR inputs at the 99th percentile reduced out-of-fold log-RMSE from 0.1263 to 0.1215. Two unusually large, low-priced houses drove the gain across both splits. Error increased slightly for the other 1,458 homes.
Use local context where it helps
We tested neighbourhood-relative features and varied the final blend by sale condition. For one unusually large test home, nearby comparable sales helped us revise the blend’s estimate. The observed submission gain from that adjustment applies to that single case.
